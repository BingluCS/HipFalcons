HIP device port by OpenAI, 2026, based on the Falcons GPU sources at
b564b454e98f732284fda6cbd194ae597c698d65.

Changes include HIP runtime translation, portable bit reading and packing,
wide prefix scans, escape-count validation, signed-zero quantization,
host-side method preflight, and an experimental 1.1.0 split-FSE decoder.
The pure GPU encoder is experimental and disabled by default; define
FGPU_ENABLE_EXPERIMENTAL_PURE_ENCODER=1 to build it for validation.
The split-FSE decoder is experimental and disabled by default; define
FGPU_ENABLE_EXPERIMENTAL_SPLIT_DECODER=1 to build it for validation.

LICENSE contains the original SZ license and disclaimer, retained verbatim.
The other files retain the upstream third-party license alternatives and notices.
